Interim Progress Meeting Report


I had my progress meeting once my internship was finished, but I thought it was still important to discuss my internship and talk about what went wrong, what parts I liked and whether the internship was valuable to me as a student.

In the last half of my internship I was becoming a bit more confident talking to my internship supervisor about the work and being able to discuss it to get good feedback. I was also focusing more on producing work for Ten Hammers that was more aligned with their needs while still making things that I am proud of. I learned how to take feedback better and reminded myself that feedback isn’t personal, it helps you improve and change your way of thinking.

In the second half of my internship I had the opportunity to do a promotional video for social media, which was something I really enjoyed doing, and I might not have had the opportunity to find out that that’s something I enjoy if not for this internship. It also gave me the opportunity to use my sound design skills in a different context, which was great. I think overall I got a lot of good content for my portfolio, and that is one of the most important parts of undertaking an internship.
